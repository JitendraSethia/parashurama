# Trainee guide

PARASHURAMA teaches one skill: when a drone appears, **detect it, identify it, and choose the right response**, fast and under pressure. Every drill has three steps: **Brief → Fight → Learn**.

## First time: do the Academy (about 10 minutes)

On the brief screen, the **Academy** card has four guided lessons. A coach on the left of the screen explains every clue in plain words, tells you what the contact is and why, and points (with a glowing outline) at the exact button to press. In lessons 1–3 the drill pauses for you at each decision. Lesson 4 gives hints only when you press **N** or get stuck.

| Lesson | You learn |
|---|---|
| 1 · Friend or bird? | What each clue means; when **not** to fire |
| 2 · Radio-controlled drones | 2.4 GHz control link = recon, 5.8 GHz video = FPV → **RF jam** |
| 3 · No radio link | No emission = jamming fails → **GNSS spoof**; one spoof hits nearby swarm drones |
| 4 · Night raid, RF detector down | Decide without one sensor; most urgent contact first |

Academy drills are practice and do not count toward your record.

## In a drill

1. **Yellow dot = unknown.** Click it to start a track. Faster = more Detect points.
2. **Read the clues** on the right as they arrive: radar, friendly check, RF signal, behaviour, camera.
3. **Identify** (keys **1–6**) and say how sure you are (**Q** Likely · **W** Confident · **E** Certain), then **Enter**.
4. **Defeat:** only weapons that can reach it are lit. **J** jam · **S** spoof · **I** interceptor · **G** gun · **H** hold.
5. Watch the coloured bars on each contact card: they show when each weapon can reach it. The red tick is impact.

Other keys: **Tab** next contact · **Space** pause · **F** field guide (Academy only) · **M** sound (off / beeps / beeps + voice callouts).

## Scoring (out of 100 per contact)

- **Detect (30):** full marks within 3 s of the dot first appearing.
- **Identify (30):** right and Certain scores most; wrong and Certain scores almost nothing. Say Certain only when the clues agree.
- **Defeat (40):** the right first choice for what the drone really is. Holding fire on friendlies and birds is correct and scores full marks.

## After the drill

- **Three lessons:** your biggest point losses and the clue you missed.
- **Drill tape and replay:** your decisions against the expert ghost on the same drill.
- **What if…:** rewind one contact, choose a different identification or weapon, and see the result.
- **Do you know what you know?** Your confidence against your accuracy.
- **Where you looked:** sectors you ignored that threats came from.

The enemy then builds your next drill around your weak spots.
